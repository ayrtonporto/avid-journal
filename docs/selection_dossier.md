#### 3. Teorema principal

**Entorno:** `\begin{theorem}` (#1 de 4 total)

```latex
\label{theor-0} Suppose that $\mathcal{H}(x)=\{\pi_1(x),\ldots,\pi_{m}(x)\}$ is a collection of hyperplanes in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Let $\mathcal{H'}\subset\mathcal{H}$ be the partition of $\mathbb{R}^n$ by $m-1$ hyperplanes and $\mathcal{H''}\subset\mathcal{H'}$ be the partition of $\mathbb{R}^{n-1}$ by $t$ hyperplanes, $t<m$. Then we have \begin{multline}\label{eq-3} G_{\mathcal{H}}(n,m) = G_{\mathcal{H'}}(n,m-1) + G_{\mathcal{H''}}(n-1,t) \leq \\ G(n,m-1) + G(n-1,m-1). \end{multline}
```
